SiempreFresco is a tool for food inventory management and waste prevention. The application organizes the information entered by the user (dates, names, codes). SiempreFresco does NOT guarantee the safety or real state of the food; the user is solely responsible for verifying if a product is fit for consumption through physical inspection (smell, sight, touch). The application includes a Shopping List that allows the user to manage the products they wish to restock; the inclusion of items in this list is manual or suggested by the app after the deletion, consumption, or donation of a product.

The app may display local reminders on your device for notices of products nearing expiration, according to the user's settings. To ensure the effectiveness of these notices, the application requires the use of exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM), which is essential for notifying the user at the precise configured time, contributing directly to food safety and the prevention of product waste. These reminders do not constitute health advice nor guarantee the fitness of the food.
